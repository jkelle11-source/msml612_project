## Paired Δ vs. base ± SE, 95% CI, verdict

A Δ is a per-seed difference of MAEs inside one regime; negative is a gain. The verdict is the ±1 SE error bar (PROJECT_PLAN §3.3): "SE-separated" if Δ ± SE clears 0, else "no significant effect". The 95% CI (t at n − 1 df) changes no verdict. With 4 df a contrast with no true effect clears ±1 SE 37% of the time, nothing here corrects for the 16 contrasts, and they share runs, so they are not independent. Gates (§3.5): mean |gate| ± SE over seeds, then each seed's signed value (42–46); a gate starts at 0, and its sign means nothing.

**M8-4:** Ext 1's block contrast (block-aware regime, block holdout) is +0.0007 ± 0.0009, 95% CI −0.0018 to +0.0031: no significant effect, so M8-4's attribution checks do not run (§4.1: a null needs no control).
